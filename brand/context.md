# Brand Context

## Brand
- **Name**: Research Desk
- **Category**: Open-source AI research agent and developer tool
- **Description**: An evidence-led research agent that plans, searches, evaluates, and writes sourced reports.
- **Stage**: Early
- **Website**: N/A

## Audience
- **Primary Audience**: AI developers evaluating agent workflows and research teams that need a transparent path from question to sourced report.
- **Key Problem**: Multi-step AI research often hides its process, loses source context, and produces polished answers without an inspectable quality loop.
- **Their Language**: ReAct loop, tool calls, evidence, sources, evaluation, observability, trace, run ID, reproducible workflow.

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**: Research Desk makes the research method visible through an explicit plan, bounded ReAct loops, evidence capture, quality evaluation, and run-level observability across TUI, API, and CLI.
- **Competitors**: OpenAI Deep Research, Perplexity, GPT Researcher
- **Market Position**: Niche, accessible open-source developer tool

## Brand Personality
- **Personality Words**: Precise, investigative, calm, transparent, technical
- **Tone**: Professional and direct; serious without sounding institutional
- **Voice Admires**: Linear, Stripe documentation, The Browser Company editorial launches

## Values & Mission
- **Core Values**: Evidence over assertion, visible process, bounded autonomy, operational clarity, useful restraint
- **Mission**: Give technical teams a research agent whose method is as credible and inspectable as its final answer.

## Goals
- **Primary Goal**: Establish Research Desk as a credible reference implementation for observable, evidence-led LangGraph research workflows.
- **Key Metrics**: Repository stars, successful setup rate, completed research runs, contributor activity, documentation engagement.
